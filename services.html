Services

Framing to museum standards

Attention to detail, reliability, expert craftsmanship and an old fashioned service.

Bespoke framing

Hand finished frames

Hand Made Frames design and manufacture unique, distinctive and high quality hand finished frames, using hundreds of specialised profiles and finishes.

We also have a huge amount of pre-manufactured mouldings on display.

Conservation

Archival protection

Conservation materials are used offering archival protection that will extend the life and beauty of any artwork. All framing is to museum standards.

Restoration

Frames & mirrors restored

Restoration of Australian and European original frames of all periods are repaired and re-gilded, adding great value to paintings.

Hand Made Frames also specialises in the restoration of mirrors.

Architectural gilding

Gilding on location

Hand Made Frames has experience working on location gilding large and small projects, including walls, doors and stairs.

Contact us for an on site consultation.
