What we do

Unique, distinctive, hand finished frames

Hand Made Frames design and manufacture high quality hand finished frames, using hundreds of specialised profiles and finishes. All framing is to museum standards.

Bespoke framing

Frames designed and made by hand, plus a huge range of pre-manufactured mouldings on display.

Conservation framing

Archival materials that extend the life and beauty of any artwork.

Restoration & gilding

Australian and European original frames of all periods repaired and re-gilded.

Mirrors & more

Mirror restoration, mirrors made to order, canvas stretching and presentation boxes.

About Mick

Over 25 years in specialist framing

Mick Howarth is originally from the United Kingdom and has worked in London, Leeds, Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne, building hundreds of large frames from scratch for museums and art galleries.

Examples of his work can be found in the Art Gallery of WA and Mick Jagger’s London apartment! He has run Hand Made Frames in Perth since 1999.
